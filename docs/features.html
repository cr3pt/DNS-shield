DNS-shield capability map

A privacy-respecting DNS ad and tracker blocker that runs entirely on your own network. Everything below ships in the box - filtering, per-client policy, anomaly detection, and the account security to let more than one person administer it safely.

DNS filtering & resolution

core

The actual blocking and answering path - what gets refused, what gets rewritten, and how a query finds its way upstream.

Blocklist filtering
Hosts, AdBlock, and plain-domain source formats, refreshed on a schedule, cached to disk for offline restarts.
Allow / deny lists
Global overrides on top of every blocklist, plus an experimental regex denylist for pattern matches.
Destination-IP blocking
Refuses an answer by the IP it resolves to, not just the domain that was asked for.
Non-Latin domain blocking
Refuses Cyrillic, Arabic, and CJK-script domains outright.
Category blocklists per group
Adult, gambling, or social-media lists scoped to one client group instead of the whole network.
Forced SafeSearch
Restricted Mode across Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, YouTube, and Yandex, network-wide or per group.
Newly Registered Domains
HaGeZi's NRD feed blocks domains registered in the last 7 days - the "baby domain" defense enterprise gateways use.
Typosquat detection
Catches close spelling variants and embedded brand names, with an option to redirect to the real address or one you specify.
DNSSEC
Sets the DO bit on upstream queries and caches the validation status per domain.
Custom DNS records
Local A/AAAA answers for your own hostnames - no upstream round trip.
Upstream routing & strategy
Per-domain and per-group upstream selection; in-order, fastest, most-stable, or race-all-at-once.
Answer caching
An in-memory, TTL-respecting cache shared by every worker process.
Pause blocking
Suspends every list network-wide for a set duration, without editing a single rule.

Clients & schedules

policy

Different rules for different devices, and for different times of day.

Client groups
Matched by IP/CIDR range, MAC address, or resolved hostname.
Bedtime schedules
Per-group time windows that block everything, independent of any blocklist.

Anomaly detection & alerts

watch

Heuristics that flag unusual traffic without sending a single query off your network.

Heuristic anomaly detection
Flags blocked-ratio spikes, domain bursts, and DNS-tunneling-shaped traffic patterns.
Alert rules
Watch specific domains or entire list sources and get notified on any hit.
Historical reanalysis
Replays past traffic against the current rules to catch what a new rule would have flagged.

Notifications

alerts out

Four independent channels, all off by default, all pointed at a service you control.

Email
Sent through your own SMTP server - not a third-party relay.
Webhooks
Discord, Slack, or a Telegram bot, each with its own minimum severity.
Browser notifications
Native OS popups while a dashboard tab is open, nothing server-side to configure.
URL ping (cron)
Plain HTTP GET to any URL on a timer, not tied to anomalies - keep-alive pings, uptime check-ins, or triggering another service on a schedule.

Accounts & security

access

Multiple administrators, several ways to prove it's really them, and a record of what each of them did.

Multi-user accounts
Admin and restricted roles, with per-user scoping to specific clients or groups.
Public view
A login-free summary - trend and top blocked domains only - safe to leave on a shared screen.
HTTPS for the dashboard
Self-signed out of the box, or point it at a certificate of your own.
Login rate limiting
Locks out an IP after repeated failed attempts, for a duration you set.
Two-factor authentication
TOTP with QR-code setup and one-time backup codes for when the device isn't at hand.
Passkeys
WebAuthn as a second factor - a hardware key or a device's own biometric unlock - alongside or instead of TOTP.
API tokens
Bearer tokens for scripts and dashboards, inheriting the permissions of the account that issued them.
Audit log
Every successful change, who made it, and which address it came from.

Administration

operate

Getting existing config in, current config out, and answers about what a query actually did.

AdGuard Home & Pi-hole import
Brings over existing filter lists, rules, and client groups from either project.
Config backup & restore
A full configuration snapshot as a downloadable zip, built entirely in memory.
Query log browser
Searchable and filterable, with a quick block/unblock action on every row.
Threat-intel enrichment
On-demand lookups against VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, OTX, or a local service you run.

Observability & network integrations

wire

Plugging DNS-shield into the rest of your infrastructure, and letting other clients speak to it encrypted.

Prometheus metrics
A /metrics endpoint covering queries, blocks, cache state, and unacknowledged anomalies.
Health endpoint
/healthz, wired directly into the Docker image's own HEALTHCHECK.
DoH & DoT upstream
Encrypts the hop from DNS-shield to whichever resolver you send queries to.
DoH & DoT listeners
DNS-shield itself answers encrypted queries from clients that ask for them.
Local block page
An explained "why was this blocked" page for null_ip mode, naming the exact rule that matched.

interface

Eight color themes

Self-contained UI
